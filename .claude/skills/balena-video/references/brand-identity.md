# Balena identity and art direction

## Locked character
Balena is a small, low, wide, chubby blue whale. The head and body form one round or oval mass. Keep a compact silhouette, small rounded fins, short tail, large black-and-white eyes, pink cheeks, a small friendly smile, and a cream belly with thin vertical blue stripes. A small brown ceramic coffee cup rests on the head; familiar blue splash shapes sit behind it. Use the chosen canonical reference for the precise splash count and cup orientation; do not add extra splashes between frames.

No neck, legs, feet, toes, shoes, fingers, long arms, long torso, upright human stance, tall body, thin whale, aggressive realism or human-shaped plush posture. Translate the reference faithfully into 2D rather than copying a 3D material. The reference sheet's generic demonstration package is not a real SKU reference.

Movement: small bob, blink, fin gesture, delighted gaze, tiny tail flick, gentle low slide or swim. No human walking. Keep the head cup attached without sliding, changing shape, filling magically or intersecting splash geometry. Do not add clothing/accessories unless requested. A Christmas hat must not replace or obscure the signature cup if explicitly approved.

## Visual system
Default: 2D anime, deliberate outlines, flat/cel-shaded volume, soft painted setting, believable spatial staging, warm emotional storytelling. Avoid 3D render, photoreal mascot, plastic shine, bulky plush texture, dark orange grade, neon saturation and heavy bloom. Keep label artwork unchanged even when the setting is drawn.

Christmas: bright ivory light, pine/sage greens, restrained cranberry red and champagne gold. Tree ornaments, soft fairy lights, ribbon, pine branches and occasional snowfall supply the season. Keep whites neutral and scene bright without clipping the white pouch. Let the mascot's blue remain consistent. Decoration frames the products; it does not cover labels or spouts.

Brand phrase: “Good Coffee. Brighter Days.” Use only when text/audio is allowed. Brand logo comes from a supplied asset, never a generated approximation. Campaign tone: warm, playful, premium and welcoming.

## Thai copy
Natural Thai, concise and specific. Match an approved CTA: “ทัก DM เพื่อสั่งซื้อ” or verified Shopee/Lazada destinations. Do not invent shop links. No health, weight-loss, caffeine, sustainability, origin or delivery claims without evidence. Tasting notes are sensory descriptors, not automatically ingredients. Whiskey and Vino Rosso are coffee product names; do not claim alcohol content. An old 5–15% campaign does not establish a new active discount.
